description: "Search the web and read pages with Exa. Use when a request needs current facts, external sources, a supplied URL, or information about people, companies, news or publications."
---

Exa is a search engine with rich structured data, including people, companies, news, publications, and more.

Search with `web_search_exa` before answering unless you're certain the answer can't have changed since your training cutoff. Use `web_fetch_exa` to read the full contents of one or more URLs.
